    How to use BodyCoach 3D

    Tap the microphone and speak. It keeps listening until you tap it again. Use the EN / हिं button to switch between English and Hindi commands. You can also tap the buttons or type a command.

    Choose who follows

    All = all three models together. Or say a name first: Priya sit, Arjun jump, Riya do push ups.

    Basic commands

    sit stand up walk run jump wave namaste turn around kneel squat bend forward bend backward hands up stand on one leg hands and knees lie on your back lie on abdomen lie on side stop

    Mouth / throat

    open your mouth show tongue show tonsils / say aah close mouth. The tonsil view has Brodsky grade 0–4 and an "inflamed" option.

    Physiotherapy

    Say a whole routine: exercise for backache, sciatica, lumbar, cervical, calf pain, knee, shoulder, body building. Or one exercise: bridge, cat cow, piriformis, chin tuck, calf raise, push ups, plank…

    Hindi examples

    बैठो खड़े हो चलो कूदो मुंह खोलो जीभ दिखाओ गला दिखाओ पीठ के बल लेटो पेट के बल लेटो कमर दर्द गर्दन रुको

    Offline voice

    Everything works without internet. For voice commands offline, Android needs its offline speech pack: Settings → Google → (Voice / Speech) → Offline speech recognition → download English (India) and Hindi.

    Camera

    Drag to rotate, pinch to zoom. The View button cycles Auto / Front / Side / Above / Top.

    Tap anywhere to close.
